# Bench validation, 2026-09-23

The phone's existing live selfie stream was 240 × 320 pixels. The pinned MediaPipe detector ran on the phone and found one face during the camera-only test. No camera frames were copied or saved.

Read-only controller preflight returned calibrated Pan (A=Left, B=Right) and Tilt (A=Down, B=Up), head support enabled, and a 150 µs/s head speed limit. One read-only request briefly timed out just after main power was restored; the next preflight succeeded. This is a connection observation, not a servo fault diagnosis.

With GrowBot stationary on the bench and the operator beside the power switch, the first small pan trial used image sign `+1`. The Pico acknowledged targets and reported reaching them, but the operator observed the head turning **away** from the face. Tracking was stopped. Repeating with pan sign `-1` moved the detected face toward image center; the operator confirmed the head physically tracked left/right. The operator also confirmed up/down tracking with tilt sign `-1`. The controller ACKs report `physical_feedback:false`; the operator observation is the evidence of real movement.

The trial was stopped and GrowBot left in the soft stationary pause with his configured head hold. No gait or body-turn commands were sent by this prototype. This is a stationary bench result only; behavior while walking, under disconnects, or with a different phone mount remains unverified.

Follow-up: GrowBot's saved head gestures remain distinct from face tracking. A priority handoff has been added to the prototype so a saved `look` command can move the head independently, and the tracker yields rather than immediately overriding it. The awake coexistence path has not been physically validated and must not be inferred from the bench tracking result.

Later powered bench checks: GrowBot's first spoken claim that face tracking was on had no new tool action, and the head did not move; narration alone was not accepted as evidence. A direct user-command bridge was then added. It started a bounded tracking window from “follow my face,” and the operator confirmed physical left/right following. Up/down initially barely moved because tilt began near the +60% tracking boundary. Camera-only observation measured face-center y across roughly 0.15–0.85 of frame height, ruling out a fixed face detector. A paced tilt-to-center command physically moved the head without strain. A second powered trial from center produced better vertical following, though the operator reported motion was somewhat slow and jerky. The subsequent pulse-scaled step and smoother face filtering changes require a fresh physical check; they are not yet a validated improvement.

Final supervised bench check: the revised controller gently centered an edge-positioned tilt before starting, used per-axis pulse-scaled steps and a short-lived 20-second tracking window, then stopped automatically. The owner confirmed that GrowBot physically followed their face and that the legs did not move. This confirms stationary tracking on this mount; it is not a walking/tracking test. Direct “follow my face” requests now enter the local tool path without starting a model reply, so a motion announcement is not required. The owner did not separately rate the revised smoothness, and the session-only motion-speech toggle was code-checked on/off but not evaluated for all of GrowBot's other autonomous motions.

Natural-attention trial, 23 September: with GrowBot awake on a bench and feet clear, the owner positioned themselves at the selfie camera. A bounded 18-second `attend` window detected one face across 40 processed frames. The Pico ACKed head targets moving commanded pan from about -0.113 to -0.287 normalized units and tilt from +0.600 to +0.328. The window auto-stopped and was explicitly stopped again. No walk or body-turn command was issued by the test. The owner reported that the head physically followed them without being asked and the legs stayed still. This is not a report of head temperature or a walking test. The walk-forward handoff has only offline mock-controller tests, not a powered walking validation.

With separate owner approval, a head-only `headForward()` call returned `commandedForward:true` and `physicalFeedback:false`. Read-only preflight then showed neutral pan and tilt targets. The owner observed the head physically returning forward/level at a comfortable pace, without leg motion or strain. This validates the centering step on the bench, not the deferred `runGait` handoff or a complete walking cycle.

Pan pacing follow-up: the owner reported choppy left/right motion both in face following and GrowBot's own saved looks. The installed Pico still reports its mechanical 150 µs/s head limit. Its native saved left/right look spans 60% of pan travel; a full opposite-side sweep can therefore take roughly eight seconds. A read-only snapshot during the complaint found a saved `look right` active, so the user was observing more than the face-tracker lane. Automatic attention was stopped for diagnosis. The new phone-side tracker computes each bounded target from the Pico ACK's current commanded PWM rather than accumulating against its previous target, and gives pan two opportunities per tilt. These changes have passed code/offline tests but not yet a new face-following physical comparison. A 30%-arc `quickLook('look left')` returned an ACK, and the owner confirmed the head physically looked left more smoothly. The complementary right test did not run because its bounded attention window had already expired; this was a test-timing issue, not a failed servo command. The head servo was reported cool before this bench comparison.

The next bounded `quickLook('look right')` was accepted from +30% toward -30%, but the Pico froze near -15% after its three-second head lease expired. The owner confirmed rightward motion followed by a premature stop. The prototype now polls `head_info` through that motion; those read-only polls renew the lease, and completion requires commanded PWM within 3 µs of the requested target. The corrected crossover has not yet been physically rechecked.

With GrowBot asleep and servo power disconnected, the installed `pico_head.py`, `stock_commands.py`, and `shared_body.py` were copied from the Pico as rollback backups and SHA-256 matched the local working source before edits. The head cap was raised to **200 µs/s** in those three local modules and their related local tests. All 102 head/shared/saved-command tests passed. Only those three modules were uploaded; read-back SHA-256 matched all three edited files. After Pico reset, a read-only head preflight over the relay reported `max_speed_us_s:200`, support enabled, and no physical feedback. The 200 µs/s physical motion is **not validated yet**; servo power remained off for upload and read-back.
